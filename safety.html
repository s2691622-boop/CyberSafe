Online Safety Tips

Simple habits can help you make safer decisions online.

🔐

Protect Your Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for important accounts. Avoid using easily guessed information.

🔑

Never Share OTPs

Never share OTPs, passwords or verification codes with another person.

🔗

Check Links

Carefully check links and website addresses before opening them or entering information.

📱

Keep Devices Updated

Keep your operating system, browser and applications updated to improve security.

🛡️

Use Two-Factor Authentication

Enable two-factor authentication on accounts whenever the option is available.

🤔

Think Before You Act

Be cautious with unexpected requests for money, information or immediate action.

✅ DO

  • Verify unexpected messages.
  • Use official websites and applications.
  • Keep your software updated.
  • Use strong passwords.
  • Think before clicking links.

❌ DON'T

  • Don't share passwords or OTPs.
  • Don't click suspicious links.
  • Don't trust unexpected prize messages.
  • Don't make payments without verification.
  • Don't give remote access to unknown people.
