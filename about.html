About CyberSafe

CyberSafe is an awareness initiative developed as part of a Community Engagement Project for B.Sc. Information Technology. The project focuses on educating users about phishing, online scams and digital fraud.

The website provides simple information, examples, safety tips and interactive activities to help users make safer decisions while using digital platforms.


Objectives

🎯

Create Awareness

Create awareness about phishing, scams and digital fraud.

🔍

Identify Scams

Help users recognize common warning signs of scams.

🔐

Promote Safety

Encourage safe and responsible digital practices.

📊

Conduct Survey

Understand people's awareness through a field survey.


Target Audience

The project is intended for students, parents, senior citizens, digital payment users, social media users and other internet users.
