ABOUT CYBER SAFETY

Why Cyber Awareness Matters?

As more people use smartphones, online banking, digital payments and social media, online scams and phishing attempts are becoming increasingly common.

CyberSafe is an awareness initiative designed to educate users about common online threats and help them make safer decisions while using digital platforms.
