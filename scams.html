Types of Online Scams

Learn the common warning signs and how to protect yourself.

🎣

Phishing

What is it?

Phishing is a type of online fraud where attackers use fake emails, messages or websites to trick people into revealing sensitive information.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected messages
  • Suspicious links
  • Urgent requests
  • Requests for sensitive information

Stay Safe

Verify the sender and website before clicking links or providing information.

💳

Payment Scams

What is it?

Payment scams involve deceptive requests that attempt to convince users to send money or approve an unexpected payment.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected payment requests
  • Pressure to act quickly
  • Unknown payment requests
  • Unusual instructions

Stay Safe

Verify the person or organization independently before making any payment.

💼

Fake Job Scams

What is it?

Fake job scams use false employment offers to obtain money or personal information from job seekers.

Warning Signs

  • Guaranteed job offers
  • Requests for advance payment
  • Unverified recruiters
  • Requests for sensitive information

Stay Safe

Research the company and verify the job opportunity through official sources.

🛒

Online Shopping Scams

What is it?

Fraudulent shopping websites or sellers may advertise unrealistic deals to deceive customers.

Warning Signs

  • Extremely low prices
  • Unknown websites
  • No reliable contact information
  • Suspicious payment requests

Stay Safe

Check the website, seller information and reviews before making a purchase.

📱

OTP Scams

What is it?

Fraudsters may attempt to trick users into revealing one-time passwords or verification codes.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected OTP requests
  • Unknown callers
  • Pressure to share codes
  • Suspicious messages

Stay Safe

Never share an OTP or verification code with another person.

📞

Fake Customer Care

What is it?

Scammers may pretend to be customer-support representatives to obtain information or money.

Warning Signs

  • Unexpected calls
  • Requests for remote access
  • Requests for sensitive information
  • Pressure to act quickly

Stay Safe

Use contact information from the organization's official website or application.
